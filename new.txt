Token Transfer Logic: Ensure that your contract can manage the transfer of tokens during the auction process, including the ability to lock tokens until the auction concludes.

Token Locking Mechanism: Code that ensures tokens are locked during the auction and only released to winning bidders after the auction ends.
Event Emissions: Emit events for bids and auction status updates, which can be useful for front-end interfaces and tracking auction progress.
Integration Functions: Functions that allow your smart contract to communicate with Uniswap's contracts, enabling seamless interactions during the auction.
